# Voice

> Universal across all owner brand surfaces. Applies to copy, UI text, error messages, social posts, video scripts, README files, and any other written artifact.

## Tone

- **Direct.** Get to the point.
- **Clear.** Prefer concrete words over abstract.
- **Slightly witty.** Earned through specificity, not jokes.
- **Confident from experience.** 27 years in the industry, earned. Never humble-brag, never credential-stack.
- **Specific and honest.** Real names, real numbers, real outcomes.
- **Never corporate.** No exec-deck language.
- **Never AI-sounding.** No buzzwords. No context framing. No mystical filler.

## DO and DON'T

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Direct and clear | Vague or corporate |
| Slightly witty | Try-hard or forced humor |
| Confident from experience | Humble-brag or credential-stacking |
| Short sentences that land | Long winding explanations |
| Specific and honest | Generic and polished |
| "27 years in the industry" | "Passionate about revolutionizing industries" |
| "The student content is free. Always." | "Our content is freely available to all learners" |
| "I build tools engineers actually use" | "I develop innovative solutions for stakeholders" |
| Use underscores for style: `Builder_` | Use em dashes — ever |
| Eyebrow labels with `//` prefix | Headers with ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation |

## Hard prohibitions

These are not preferences. They are non-negotiable across every artifact.

### No em dashes
Zero em dashes anywhere. Use underscores, colons, semicolons, parentheses, or rewrite. This applies to:

- Website copy
- UI text
- Error messages
- Email
- Social posts
- Video scripts
- Code comments
- Commit messages
- Documentation
- Form placeholders

If a sentence feels like it needs an em dash, rewrite it.

### No AI buzzwords
Banned phrases include but are not limited to:

- "Elevate your X"
- "Built for modern teams"
- "The future of Y"
- "Streamline your workflow"
- "Powered by intelligence"
- "Unlock the power of"
- "Take your X to the next level"
- "Reimagine your Z"
- "Seamlessly"
- "Results-driven"
- "Innovative solutions"
- "Stakeholders"
- "Revolutionize"
- "Synergize"
- Anything with "context framing" preamble ("In today's fast-paced world...")

### No mystical / oracular filler
Two-word epigrams used to fill subtitle space without saying anything. Examples to avoid: "Seek. Knock. Enter.", "Anno Lucis", "Stay the course." Unless the subject domain genuinely earns it (and even then, sparingly).

### No corporate hedge language
- "We strive to..."
- "Our mission is to..."
- "Committed to..."
- "Dedicated to..."

State the thing directly. If you do something, say you do it. Don't say you "strive to" do it.

## Brand-mark conventions

- **Trailing underscore wordmark.** Brand phrases close with `_`. Examples: `Michael Collins_`, `Solutions Wizard_`, `Builder_`, `By Michael_`. The underscore is the visual signature.
- **`//` eyebrow prefix.** Section headers and labels use `//` as a comment-style eyebrow. Renders via CSS `::before { content: '//' }` with cyan color.
- **Sign-off.** "Stay classy. Keep pushing boundaries." or "Stay on the level." for personal projects.

## Voice testing

Before shipping any copy, ask:

1. Could this have been written by ChatGPT cold? If yes, rewrite.
2. Does it have specifics (numbers, names, real outcomes)? If no, add them or cut.
3. Does it sound like *me* talking, or like a brand ad? If the latter, rewrite.
4. Are there em dashes? If yes, fix.
5. Is there a banned phrase? If yes, fix.

Voice is enforced at delivery, not asked about during intake.
