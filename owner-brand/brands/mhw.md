# Material Handling Weekly Brand

> The MHW newsletter / publication brand. Professional, editorial, industry-publication feel. Navy and gold only.
>
> **Source:** [`../source/MichaelCollins_BrandGuide.docx`](../source/MichaelCollins_BrandGuide.docx)
> **Drop-in tokens:** [`../tokens/mhw.css`](../tokens/mhw.css)

## When to use this brand

- materialhandlingweekly.com
- MHW Beehiiv newsletter (Tradecraft template)
- MHW social assets
- MHW-specific cards / strips on the personal site (`btn-gold` / MHW callouts)
- Any property under the Material Handling Weekly umbrella

**Do not use for:** the personal site at large, internal tools, client work, or any project not specifically MHW.

## Color system

Navy and gold only. No additional accents. The restraint is the brand.

| Swatch | Name | Role | Usage |
|--------|------|------|-------|
| `#1A1A2E` | MHW Navy | Primary / Core | All backgrounds, navbars, headers. |
| `#F0C419` | MHW Gold | Accent | Buttons, highlights, links, subscribe CTAs. The defining brand color. |
| `#E6EDF3` | MHW Light | Body Text | Main text color on dark backgrounds. |
| `#FFFFFF` | White | Secondary Text | Headers and emphasis text. |

### Semantic color rules (strict)

- **Navy** is the only background. No alternatives.
- **Gold** is the only accent. No alternatives.
- **Cyan, violet, blue** from the personal-site brand are **not** used in MHW contexts.
- This restraint is the brand's identity. Adding accents dilutes it.

## Typography

Selected within the Beehiiv Tradecraft template.

| Font | Role | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Archivo | Primary — all text | Clean, modern, technical. Default for MHW. |
| Montserrat | Alternative | Bolder option, also works for MHW headers. |

### Type voice

- Editorial, industry-publication. Closer to a trade journal than a SaaS marketing site.
- Headlines are restrained, not display-dramatic.
- Body text is comfortable for long-form reading.
- Letter-spacing default. No aggressive negative tracking on MHW.

## Design rules

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| Navy and gold only | No additional accent colors. The restraint is the brand. |
| Editorial restraint | Avoid display-dramatic headlines. Closer to trade journal than to product marketing. |
| Subscribe CTAs are gold | The gold subscribe button is the dominant CTA pattern. |
| Long-form readable | Body text comfortable for newsletter-length reading. |
| No glass effects | The personal-site glass card aesthetic does not apply to MHW. Solid surfaces. |
| No custom cursor | The personal-site custom cursor does not apply. |
| Eyebrows: optional, sparingly | The personal-site `//` eyebrow pattern is optional in MHW; if used, restrained. |

## Voice

Universal voice rules in [`../voice.md`](../voice.md). MHW carries voice with industry-publication framing — still direct, still confident, still specific. Slightly more editorial in cadence than personal-site copy.
